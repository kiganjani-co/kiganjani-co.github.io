Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Kiganjani Co. ("we", "us") operates this website to provide information about our digital agency services. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information you send us

When you use the enquiry form, we collect your name, email address, business name (if provided), phone number (if provided), and the content of your message. We use this information only to respond to your enquiry and, with your consent, to follow up about our services.

2. Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics, which collects anonymised, aggregate traffic data (such as page views and approximate region). Vercel Analytics does not use cookies and does not track individual visitors across sites.

3. Third parties

Enquiry submissions are stored securely on our hosting provider's infrastructure. We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

4. Retention

We keep enquiry records only as long as needed to respond to and manage your enquiry.

5. Your rights

You may ask us to correct or delete the personal information you have submitted by emailing kiganjani.co@gmail.com.
